Budgeting

What affects the cost of a kitchen remodel?

Every kitchen is different, so every estimate is too. These are the factors that most often move the number up or down — we’ll walk through each one with you during your free consultation.

01

Kitchen size & layout changes

Moving walls, relocating the sink or range, and expanding the footprint all add engineering and labor time.

02

Cabinet & countertop material

Stock cabinetry and laminate counters cost less than custom cabinetry and natural stone.

03

Appliance & fixture upgrades

New appliance packages, smart fixtures, and specialty lighting add to material costs.

04

Plumbing, electrical & permits

Relocating plumbing or upgrading electrical service requires permitting and licensed trade work.

Kitchen Remodeling FAQ

Common questions about kitchen renovations.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A cabinet refacing or countertop-only project can wrap up in one to two weeks. A full gut renovation with layout changes, new plumbing and electrical, and custom cabinetry typically runs six to ten weeks depending on scope and material lead times.

Do I need a permit to remodel my kitchen in Nassau County?

Cosmetic updates like new cabinets or countertops in the same footprint usually don’t require a permit. Moving plumbing, electrical, or load-bearing walls does. We handle permitting and inspections for every project that needs it.

Can you move plumbing and electrical during a kitchen renovation?

Yes. Relocating a sink, range, or island often means moving plumbing and electrical runs. Our licensed trade partners handle that work to code, and we coordinate the entire schedule so it doesn’t hold up the rest of the project.

What’s the difference between cabinet refacing and full replacement?

Refacing keeps your existing cabinet boxes and replaces only the doors, drawer fronts, and hardware — faster and less expensive. Full replacement is right when you’re changing the layout or the boxes are worn out or the wrong size for a new design.
